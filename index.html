Admission counselling strategy

A NEET-style workflow: profile → choice building → saving → locking → round strategy. Once locked, the list behaves like a final submitted counselling set.

Round 1

1

Fill every college you would genuinely accept. Do not filter only by probability. Keep dream colleges first, then strategic stretch options, then safe backups.

Round 2

2

Re-open only if the round movement changes your viable zone. Otherwise keep the order stable. Reordering after emotional panic is how good seats get wasted.

Mop-up

3

Add stronger backups, especially if you still need a seat. In mop-up, completeness matters more than elegance. Empty slots are self-sabotage.

Final lock

4

Lock only after verifying documents, category status, budget ceiling, and acceptable distance. Once locked, treat it as operationally final for the round.

Strategy note: Build the list in true preference order, not probability order. The algorithm does not reward fear-based ranking. It rewards honest preference plus breadth.
Fit note: The current profile is being interpreted as a Karnataka-domicile candidate with a high score band. That means government colleges should lead the list; private colleges become contingency options only if the budget is flexible.
